Profiles

A profile is a routing subject: it owns the models and providers the router may choose from, how it routes over them, the circuit breaker that protects it, and the keys that call it. Open a card for its routing rules, its model pool and its keys. Agent infrastructure — skills, memory, cron — lives in the Hermes dashboard, not here.
